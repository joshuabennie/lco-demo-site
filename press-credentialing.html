Press Credentialing

Information for journalists and media organizations seeking legislative media identification badges for access to designated Capitol press areas.

Media Identification Badges

Credentialing Overview

All applications are reviewed and must be approved before badges are issued. Legislative media identification badges are a privilege and are issued as a security measure to salaried journalists working for a commercial or non-profit news outlet providing routine nonpartisan coverage.

The outlet must be recognized by the Louisiana Broadcasters Association, the Louisiana Press Association (Active Membership, Individual Associate Membership, or Associate Online Membership only), and/or the National Press Foundation or a similar organization. The news outlet must also verify the applicant’s employment.

Eligibility & Use

Applicants cannot engage in lobbying, advocacy, advertising, publicity, or promotions for any individual, political party, corporation, business, or government agency.

Media identification badges may expedite entry into the building and may give journalists access to designated press areas during proceedings of the Legislature on a first-come, first-served basis when space is available.

Press or media badges issued by any other entity are not valid for entrance to designated media areas of the Capitol.

Cost & Pickup

The cost of a new yearly identification badge is $10.00 per badge.

The replacement cost for a lost badge is also $10.00.

Once approval is granted, payment can be made by credit card during pickup or by check/money order made out to Legislative Budgetary Control.

No cash accepted. Approved badges are available for payment and pickup in the Capitol Security Office, Room 207, on the 2nd floor.

Important Notice

You will be notified upon approval or denial of your application. Media identification badges may be revoked at any time for non-compliance with all requirements, violation of chamber rules, disruptive behavior, or failure to continue meeting qualification standards.

Other Important Information

  • Media badges do not exempt journalists or photographers from passing through security.
  • Balcony seating is open and available for overflow media and the general public.
  • Media badges are not required to monitor session proceedings from either the Senate or House balcony.
  • Cameras, both still and video, are not allowed in either chamber’s balconies.
  • Official legislative livestreaming is available through the House, Senate, and LA LEGE app platforms.
  • All other forms of streaming are prohibited in both chambers and committee rooms.
  • Journalists given access to Chamber Floor Seats receive special identification and chamber rules that must be strictly followed.
  • No photos are allowed from the chamber floor seats by the media.
  • No interviews are to be conducted on the floor of the chambers.
  • Journalists covering the Legislature must follow each chamber’s dress code.
  • Documentary filming during proceedings requires special permission and is not covered by a media badge.
