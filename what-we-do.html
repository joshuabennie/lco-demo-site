Legislative Communications Office

What We Do

LCO offers a broad range of communication, media, design, and digital services to support the work of the Louisiana Legislature and its members. Our team provides direct support to legislative offices, chambers, committees, and partner entities across a variety of service areas.

Committee Coverage

Capturing hearings, briefings, and legislative work in progress.

Press & Public Information

Supporting clear communication between the Legislature, media, and public.

Senate Chamber

Documenting the work of Louisiana’s upper chamber.

House Chamber

Sharing the work of the people’s House.

Communication & Public Affairs

Messaging, media relationships, and public-facing communication support for legislative offices.

News Writing & Editing

Professional writing support to help legislators clearly communicate ideas, updates, and public information.

Press Conferences & Media Briefings

Coordination and communication support for legislative press events, public statements, and media outreach.

News Monitoring

Real-time news tracking and distribution to keep members informed on relevant coverage and developments.

Creative & Outreach Services

Design, print, and engagement services that help offices reach constituents more effectively.

Graphic Design

Custom visual design for publications, campaigns, informational materials, and legislative communications.

Direct Mail Marketing

Development of mailers, surveys, brochures, pamphlets, and other print communication pieces.

Constituency Outreach

Support for building stronger public engagement and communication opportunities with Louisiana citizens.

Media Production & Digital Services

Technical, visual, and online communication support across legislative platforms and proceedings.

Photography & Videography

Coverage of legislative meetings, events, and other official activities through dedicated visual media support.
