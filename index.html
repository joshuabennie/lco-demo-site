Legislative Communications Office

Advancing the Louisiana Legislature through communication, media, design, and digital support.

The mission of the Legislative Communications Office is to advance the Louisiana Legislature by helping its members and staff gather, consider, and fully discuss ideas; increase public engagement; foster confidence; and build goodwill.

We support both chambers with non-partisan communication strategy, public information services, creative production, and digital outreach built for modern legislative engagement.

The Legislative Communications Office helps Louisiana lawmakers and staff connect with the public through clear messaging, creative services, video and audio production, digital publishing, and statewide media support.
