Our Mission

The mission of the Legislative Communications Office is to advance the Louisiana Legislature by helping its members and staff gather, consider, and fully discuss ideas; increase public engagement; foster confidence; and build goodwill.

We support both chambers with non-partisan communication strategy, public information services, creative production, and digital outreach built for modern legislative engagement.

Press Credentialing

What We Do

LCO offers a broad range of communication, creative, media, and digital services designed to support legislative offices and improve public access to information.

Communication & Public Affairs

Messaging, media relationships, and public-facing communication support for legislative offices.

News Writing & Editing

Professional writing support to help legislators clearly communicate ideas, updates, and public information.

Press Conferences & Media Briefings

Coordination and communication support for legislative press events, public statements, and media outreach.

News Monitoring

Real-time news tracking and distribution to keep members informed on relevant coverage and developments.

Creative & Outreach Services

Design, print, and engagement services that help offices reach constituents more effectively.

Graphic Design

Custom visual design for publications, campaigns, informational materials, and legislative communications.

Direct Mail Marketing

Development of mailers, surveys, brochures, pamphlets, and other print communication pieces.

Constituency Outreach

Support for building stronger public engagement and communication opportunities with Louisiana citizens.

Media Production & Digital Services

Technical, visual, and online communication support across legislative platforms and proceedings.

Photography & Videography

Coverage of legislative meetings, events, and other official activities through dedicated visual media support.

Audio / Video Management

Oversight of technical media systems and support for the Capitol’s camera, audio, and cable operations.

Video Streaming & Web Design

Management of legislative web experiences and online streaming for chambers, committees, and partner entities.

Public Education & Access

Programs and services that help the public, schools, and media better connect with the Legislature.

Civic Education

Support for educational outreach that helps students understand government, civics, and the legislative process.

Press Credentialing

Credentialing services for working members of the news media covering legislative proceedings.
