Legislative Communications Office

Clearer Communication for the Louisiana Legislature.

The Legislative Communications Office provides nonpartisan media, design, digital, and communication support for members and staff.

Our work helps the Legislature explain its work, engage the public, and make legislative information easier to access.

Featured Digital Resource

The Official LALEGE App

Get quick access to Louisiana legislative information from your phone. The app allows users to navigate the State Capitol, review legislative websites and resources, and stay informed during session with updates and meeting notifications.
